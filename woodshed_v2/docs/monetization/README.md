## Offer and editorial boundary

The Edge Report is an independent Woodshed publication. Its value is the process: role changes → opportunity evidence → counterevidence → what to watch. No promised wins, certainty, or “locks.”

- FFToday commissioned work remains within FFToday’s assignment, schedule, and editorial terms.
- Do not paste, lightly rewrite, bundle, or resell FFToday article text, unpublished drafts, exclusive assignment research, or FFToday-only deliverables in the newsletter, Woodshed Pro, X, VERSUS, or a client packet.
- Build newsletter conclusions independently from permitted sources. Keep citations and timestamps. If an angle overlaps an assignment, use separate source notes and distinct analysis; if rights or timing are unclear, omit it until Jon resolves the issue.
- Label assets as FFToday-commissioned, Woodshed-original, client-commissioned, or licensed/third-party. Record reuse rights and approval.
- Label sponsored or affiliate material near the placement; it cannot influence independent analysis. FTC guidance says disclosures should be clear, conspicuous, and close to affiliate recommendations.
